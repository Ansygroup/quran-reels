How the bearing is computed

The Qibla is the great-circle path from your position to the Kaaba (21.4225° N, 39.8262° E), not a flat-map line. The tool applies the standard initial-bearing formula to your coordinates, so results differ correctly between, for example, Cairo, London, Jakarta and New York.

Sample bearings

  • Cairo ≈ 136° · London ≈ 119° · Jakarta ≈ 295° · New York ≈ 58° · Dubai ≈ 258°

Hold the phone flat, align the on-screen arrow with the bearing shown, and the highlighted direction points to the Kaaba. Calibration tip: move your phone in a figure-eight if the compass drifts.

Frequently asked questions

Does the Qibla change with my city?

Yes — the bearing depends on your coordinates; the tool recomputes it for wherever you are.

Why does my mosque face a slightly different direction?

Differences of one or two degrees come from rounding conventions or building alignment; the great-circle bearing here is the mathematical reference.

Does it work without internet?

Yes — after the first load the compass works offline.

How is distance to Makkah calculated?

By the Haversine great-circle distance from your coordinates to the Kaaba, shown in kilometres.

Why Quran clips spread

Algorithms favour content that grips the viewer in the first moment: a striking recitation, one complete ayah, and a background that doesn't compete with the text. Rules that work for Islamic short clips

  • First 3 seconds — open on the heart of the ayah; no intro screen.
  • 15–60 seconds — the range short platforms push most.
  • One complete meaning — never cut an ayah away from its context.
  • Readable text — large type, high contrast, nothing stacked on the letters.
  • Caption properly — short description plus the surah name and number.

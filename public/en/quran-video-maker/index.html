Publishing tips

  • First three seconds — open on the strongest phrase of the ayah, no cold intro.
  • 15–60 seconds — one complete thought beats a cut-off half verse.
  • Name the surah in the caption so viewers can find the full context.
  • Be consistent — one clip daily outperforms five clips once a week.

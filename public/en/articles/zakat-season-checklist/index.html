The Zakat Season Checklist: Ready Before It's Due | Quran Reels

Who inventories early pays with certainty and calm; who postpones juggles estimates and delays a duty that should be light.

  • List what you own at the start
  • Confirm nisab with a method you trust
  • Decide recipients before the money sits

Inventory, then compute

Most zakat anxiety comes from not knowing one's own totals. A written inventory turns fog into figures within an hour.

Group assets simply: cash, gold/silver, trade goods, investments — then compute the net.

Deciding distribution early

Knowing eligible categories beforehand prevents hesitation when the payment arrives. Intent plus evidence equals calm charity.

Set a yearly reminder in the Hijri calendar so the checklist repeats itself automatically.

Realistic Hifz Goals: Achievable, Not Decorative | Quran Reels

An ambitious goal without a plan is scheduled disappointment; one specific monthly target with a weekly indicator builds real rhythm.

  • One monthly target (a surah or page count)
  • A weekly indicator that shows direction
  • Monthly adjustment without shame

Goals that instruct

A useful goal tells you what to do tomorrow morning. If it only impresses when spoken, it is decoration.

Weekly indicators turn a month into four chances to steer rather than one final verdict.

Adjusting honestly

At month's end, ask: capacity or discipline? If capacity was wrong, lower the number; if discipline was wrong, lower friction first.

Goals should stretch like a muscle, not snap like a rope.

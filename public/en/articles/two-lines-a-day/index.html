Two Lines a Day: The Small Number That Never Stops | Quran Reels

Small and steady defeats large and abandoned; two real lines daily become a year of surprising totals without heroics.

  • Two genuine lines, not decorative ones
  • Tally the week every Thursday
  • No raising the minimum until week four

Why tiny wins long-term

Grand plans break on ordinary days; the two-line minimum survives travel, illness, and deadlines because it asks almost nothing.

A year of unbroken small days is worth more than three heroic weeks and a silent rest of the year.

Keeping it honest

'Two lines' means recited, checked, and reviewable — not glanced at. The weekly tally keeps quality visible.

Raise the floor only when it has been effortless for a month — progress by accretion, not by sudden ambition.

Start today

Commit to the two-lines-a-day rule for thirty days before any increase.

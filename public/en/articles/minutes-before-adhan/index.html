Khushuu starts before you bow: who steadies five minutes before the adhan enters prayer with a present heart.

Arriving before the call

The adhan announces a meeting already prepared for. Wudu done, phone down, mind collected — the first rak'ah benefits immediately.

Preparation is also practical: rows fill calmly when no one arrives mid-sprint.

A three-minute sequence

Wudu check, screen off, intention sentence, join. Repeat identically every day so the sequence itself induces calm.

Anything longer can wait — depth belongs inside the prayer, not in elaborate pre-games.

Start today

Set an alert five minutes before the minutes before the adhan for quiet preparation.

FAQ

What if I am swamped until the call?

Shut the device quickly and join the row — presence can still be chosen.

A specific phrase?

The welcome du'a for the adhan is enough.

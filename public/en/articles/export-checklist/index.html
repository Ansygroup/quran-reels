The Pre-Export Checklist for Quran Videos | Quran Reels

Most publishing mistakes are made before the export button: a mistyped ayah, a clipped edge, an audio jump. A checklist turns vigilance into habit — five items, two minutes, every time. Export checklist saves the file locally, so everything on this list must be right before it leaves the page.

  • Text first: read the ayah letter by letter against the mushaf image.
  • Edges second: nothing important in the outer tenth of the frame.
  • Sound third: first and last ten seconds at the same listening level.

The five-item list

1) Ayah verified letter by letter. 2) Reciter name spelled consistently. 3) Safe zones clear of the UI overlay. 4) Audio level steady between opening and end. 5) Background matches the verse's mood, not your mood.

Run the items in the same order every time — a fixed order is what makes a checklist fast. When one item fails, fix it before export; never after upload.

The phone test

After export, open the file on your phone — not the editor — and watch it once at arm's length, once close. Look for two things: whether the ayah reads at normal distance, and whether anything distracts in the first three seconds.

Listen on the phone speaker too. What sounds rich on headphones can vanish in a loud room. If the file survives the phone test, it is ready for the platform.

Start today

Save this five-item list next to your Export checklist tab and run it on your next clip end to end. Note which item fails most often — that is your personal bottleneck to fix first.

FAQ

How long should the checklist take?

Two minutes once the order is fixed. If it takes longer, the failure is in production, not in checking.

Do I re-check old clips before republishing?

Yes — republished clips get the full list plus a fresh look at the description.

What about the thumbnail frame?

Pick it during the phone test: pause on the most readable frame and use it as the cover.

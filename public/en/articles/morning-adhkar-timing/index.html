The window and why it matters

The morning carries a blessed span from Fajr to sunrise; adhkar there ride the day's opening rather than lag behind it.

Understanding the span removes hesitation — you always know your next move.

Making it survive real mornings

Attach it to Fajr's end, keep a core set for hard days, and measure the week, not each morning.

Mercy over rigidity: a lighter consistent version beats a perfect abandoned one.

Start today

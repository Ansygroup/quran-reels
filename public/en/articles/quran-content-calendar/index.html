The Quran Content Calendar | Quran Reels

A calendar earns its place only if you open it weekly. The usable version is a month grid with four recurring slots (new, series, observance, republish), markers for the Islamic dates that matter, and a five-minute Friday review. Content calendar produces the clips; the calendar decides when they leave.

  • Four recurring slots so every week has a shape before content exists.
  • Islamic dates marked a month ahead — no mid-month surprises.
  • Five-minute Friday review: confirm next week or move deliberately.

Laying out the month

Draw the month with four slot types: (1) new piece, (2) continuing series, (3) observance or seasonal, (4) scheduled republication. Assign slots before you know the topics — the shape comes first, the content fills it.

Mark Islamic calendar dates that matter to your audience directly on the grid. Seeing a milestone two weeks out is what separates preparation from improvisation.

The Friday review

Five minutes: does next week have all four slots covered? Move something deliberately if not — a considered move is fine, a silent gap is how calendars die.

Once a month, review last month's three metrics (retention, sources, follows) and write one adjustment for the month ahead. One adjustment — a calendar full of reforms is a fantasy.

Start today

Draw next month's grid with the four slots in Content calendar's planner (or a paper calendar), mark the Islamic dates, and run your first Friday review this week.

FAQ

Paper or app?

Whichever you will open on Friday — the best calendar is the visible one, not the feature-rich one.

What if inspiration arrives off-schedule?

Put it in the next open slot; the grid bends for good ideas but must be consulted, not ignored.

How far ahead do I plan?

One month detailed, one month sketched — enough structure to prepare, enough flexibility to stay alive.

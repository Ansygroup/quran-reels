Finding the Qibla Anywhere in Two Steps | Quran Reels

The qibla is not a feeling — it is an angle on a sphere. Qibla direction computes the bearing and distance from your coordinates to Mecca, and once you align it in your room, the hotel-lobby panic disappears for the rest of your stay.

  • Results show the bearing from true north plus the distance in kilometers.
  • Your phone compass follows your body if the sensor is available.
  • While traveling, compute on arrival night — not at Fajr.

Align once, keep the mark

Face the displayed angle, then place a fixed reference — a small mark on the wall, the headboard direction, or a note with the number. From then on you do not re-open the calculator for every prayer.

In rooms without a usable compass, save the numeric bearing and use a ruler edge or floor line to transfer the angle.

What changes and what never does

The qibla depends on your coordinates, not on the city name in a dropdown — dense cities can differ slightly between districts, which is why coordinates matter.

Prayer itself remains flexible when space is tight: do what is available, facing the closest you can manage, and keep the app for exactness when the room allows it.

Start today

Open Qibla direction in your current room, align one reference, and test it tonight with a single prayer. After that, the tool becomes a travel utility, not a daily search.

FAQ

Does the qibla change between neighboring countries?

Slightly, with coordinates. That tiny drift is exactly why the page computes from your position rather than a country default.

Does it work without internet?

After the first load, the last computed result remains usable for a while; save the number before a flight.

What if I cannot face it exactly?

Make things easy: do what the space allows. The calculator is for precision when precision is possible.

A Roadmap for Missed Prayers: Steady Without Overwhelm | Quran Reels

Outstanding prayers are a debt settled by a modest steady installment — not by postponed regret nor by an exhausting sprint.

  • Estimate the amount with a clear table
  • Divide it into a small daily installment
  • Anchor the installment after a fixed prayer

Turning a weight into a plan

Vague burdens grow in the dark; a written number with a schedule becomes workable. Estimate honestly and divide without heroics.

A daily installment keeps the debt visible without dominating your day.

Anchoring the installment

Place it right after a prayer you never miss so it inherits that prayer's stability. Review the pace weekly and adjust once if needed.

Slower steady repayment still beats fast abandoned attempts.

Start today

Write in the roadmap for making up prayers your estimate, your daily installment, and its weekly check-in.

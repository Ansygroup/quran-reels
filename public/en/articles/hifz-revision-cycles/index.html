Hifz without revision is a roof without sealing; spaced cycles catch forgetting while it is still cheap.

  • Same-day revision in the evening
  • Weekly at one week, two weeks

The forgetting curve, practically

Memory fades fastest just after learning; a review the same day halves the loss before it compounds.

Each successful cycle buys a longer next interval — effort drops as security rises.

A calendar that runs itself

Sunday: last week's items. Month-end: the month. Simple repeating tasks in your calendar make cycles unmissable.

When overloaded, shrink each cycle's depth but keep its date — presence over volume.

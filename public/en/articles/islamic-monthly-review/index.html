A Short Monthly Review: Awareness, Not Audit | Quran Reels

Months pass without a pause; ten minutes of honest review reveal the pattern early enough to steer — before drift becomes identity.

  • Three things that held, one that slipped
  • Exactly one change for next month
  • Fix the next review date before finishing

Why monthly, not daily

Daily tracking invites obsession; monthly sees trends without drowning you in noise. The month is long enough for patterns and short enough for correction.

A fixed calendar night makes the practice self-keeping.

The ten-minute format

Held? Slipped? One change? Ask in that order, write three lines, and set the next date.

End with a small celebration of what held — reinforcement works better than reproach.

Start today

Add the short monthly review to a fixed night each month and keep it to ten minutes.

Rebuild the targets — review exists for adjustment, not blame.

What headphones reveal

Isolation exposes consonants, stops, and breaths that speakers smooth over — exactly the details pronunciation training needs.

They also let you review anywhere without losing fidelity.

Healthy habits

Moderate volume, regular breaks, and one clean pair dedicated to listening keep the tool pleasant long-term.

Use speakers for casual atmosphere and headphones for focused review.

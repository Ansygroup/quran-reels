A Time Buffer Around Salah: Never Pray in a Rush | Quran Reels

Rush strips salah of its presence; small margins around each time turn the adhan from a stressor into a welcome.

  • Five-minute wudu window before the adhan
  • No meetings inside the adhan window
  • Prepare clothes and space early

Margins create presence

The gap between activity and prayer is where your heart arrives before your body. Even five quiet minutes change the prayer you end up praying.

Corners of buffer also absorb the day's overruns without sacrificing salah.

Building the buffer

Map your day against the five adhans and mark no-go windows. Add a pre-alert for wudu and keep clothes ready the night before.

Treat the buffer as an appointment with God that others can see on your calendar.

Start today

Add two reminders to the buffer around every prayer: 'prepare for wudu' and 'adhan', five minutes apart.

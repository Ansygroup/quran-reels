Niche or Broad? Charting Your Quran Channel's Path | Quran Reels

Beginnings reward a tight niche people can name at once; premature variety scatters the message — balance comes from knowing your audience.

  • Start with a center a visitor can describe
  • Expand only when your audience asks for the next step
  • Do not change direction before three months of data

Why focus wins early

A narrow, legible channel earns follows because viewers understand exactly what they are subscribing to. Clarity converts.

Depth in one lane also builds authority that a mixed feed cannot.

When to widen

Expansion is safe when your audience's own requests point beyond your current scope and the new content still sounds like you.

Test extensions as occasional episodes; promote to a pillar only after they hold their own.

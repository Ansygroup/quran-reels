Preparing for Ramadan a Month Early | Quran Reels

Those who plan a month early enter Ramadan with their affairs arranged; those who plan on the first night trade their month for improvisation.

  • Lock the revision/khatma plan before the month
  • Adjust sleep in the final week, not the final night
  • Write a realistic daily deeds list

Why a month matters

Readiness compounds: a settled sleep plan, a revised khatma, and a cleared schedule each remove a thief of Ramadan hours.

Early planning also lowers spiritual procrastination — intention converts to arrangement while the gate is wide open.

Three preparations

Plan the Quran (what and how much), plan the body (sleep and food rhythm), plan the obligations (work and family expectations).

Review the plan one week before — most first drafts need one honest trimming.

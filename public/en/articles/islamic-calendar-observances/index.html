Observances on the Islamic Calendar: What to Prepare | Quran Reels

The Islamic year has a handful of moments that reliably matter to your audience — and dozens that do not. Selecting four to six observances, preparing one week ahead, and keeping your own worship separate from the content calendar prevents both burnout and hollow posts. Calendar observances is the map; your discipline is the selection.

  • Select four to six observances a year — the rest are noise.
  • Prepare one week ahead: one preview, one main, one recap each.
  • Your worship plan stays personal; the content plan stays practical.

Selecting the observances

Criteria: does my audience care, can I add something true and useful, and does it fit my formats? If two of three fail, skip it this year — presence is not obligation.

Write the shortlist with dates and a one-line purpose per entry ("first ten of Dhū al-Ḥijjah — daily action clips"). Purposes prevent the mid-season panic of not knowing what to make.

Preparing without collapsing

One week ahead: batch-produce the preview and main pieces, leave the recap for after the event so it reflects what actually happened. Batching beats daily improvisation and leaves room for your own observance.

If an observance overlaps a busy period, scale the set down to one main piece. One honest clip serves the occasion better than four rushed ones.

FAQ

Do I need to post on every observance?

No — a selected shortlist with honest coverage outperforms daily presence across dates you do not actually serve.

What about dates where rulings differ?

Follow your trusted scholars for your own practice; for content, present the reliable common ground and attribute differences plainly.

How do I avoid repeating last year's posts?

Keep titles but refresh angles and footage; a yearly series benefits from recognizable structure.

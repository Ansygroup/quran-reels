Stacking Salah Habits Onto Your Daily Routine | Quran Reels

Existing habits carry new ones; attaching prayer to fixed moments in your day outperforms depending on motivation.

  • Attach each prayer to a preceding fixed event
  • Remove the physical friction (water, mat, alert)
  • Start with the prayer closest to now

Anchors beat resolutions

Resolutions live in feelings; anchors live in events. The kettle boiling, the laptop closing, the door entering — reliable signals trigger reliable action.

Design the environment once and let it remind you daily.

Stacking in practice

Choose the target prayer, name its anchor, remove one obstacle, and rehearse the pair for a week. Then extend to the next prayer.

If the stack keeps failing, shrink it — the attachment must be easier than forgetting.

Start today

Pick one prayer from stacking salah onto your routines and bind it to a daily action you never skip.

FAQ

What if my schedule changes?

The anchor moves with it — the attachment is what stays.

How many anchors at once?

One until stable, then add the next.

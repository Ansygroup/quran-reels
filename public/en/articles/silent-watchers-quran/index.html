Silent Viewers: Why People Watch Quran Reels Without Sound | Quran Reels

Many watch in quiet places; clear text gives them the message while the recitation gives listeners their atmosphere — the two do not conflict.

  • Large, high-contrast text that reads silently
  • Place the key word near the center where UI does not cover it
  • Never rely on sound alone to carry meaning

The sound-off majority

A large share of feed watching happens muted — in transit, in bed, in public. Silent viewers do not dislike audio; their context forbids it.

Text that reads at a glance converts that context into attention instead of lost views.

Designing for both audiences

Give silent viewers the structure through text and give listeners the atmosphere through recitation. Each audience gets a complete experience.

Test both modes before publishing — sound on, then sound off.

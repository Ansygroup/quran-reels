Silencing the Phone Around Salah: Practical Guardrails | Quran Reels

A nearby phone steals presence even untouched; physical distance works better than any good intention.

  • Scheduled silent around each prayer
  • Keep the phone out of the prayer room
  • Allow genuine emergencies through priority calls only

Why distance beats discipline

The phone's mere presence activates background alertness. Removing it from the room removes the need to resist at all.

The prayer mat and the feed should never share the same square meter.

Guardrails that stick

Schedule silent mode against prayer times, whitelist real contacts, and park the phone in another room during prayers.

Tell family about the whitelist so silence never means unreachable.

Start today

Add to silencing the phone around salah: phone on silent and face-down three minutes before the adhan.

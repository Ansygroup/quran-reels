Audio Self-Check: Record, Compare, Correct | Quran Reels

Memory can flatter you; audio cannot. A recorded comparison exposes 'I think I know it' verses in one pass.

  • Record once without the text in front
  • Compare only the doubtful words against a reference
  • Fix the seam immediately, not next week

Why the ear is honest

Reading internally smooths over gaps that speaking aloud forces into the open — hesitation, substitutions, and silently skipped words all surface.

Your own voice also builds the performance confidence recitation eventually requires.

The comparison ritual

Record → listen once → mark failures → compare with a reference → re-record only the fixes. Twenty minutes, weekly.

Saved recordings double as a progress archive that motivates more than any chart.

Start today

Make the audio self-check a weekly recorded review and keep the files to hear progress.
